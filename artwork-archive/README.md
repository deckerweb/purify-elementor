# Purify Elementor – three artwork concepts

[Deutsch](README.de.md)

1. **Clear:** Broom and sparkles; plum and mint. Directly illustrates cleanup.
2. **Focus:** Filter and checkmark; petrol and turquoise. Illustrates selective filtering.
3. **Order:** Organized tiles and checkmark; forest green and lime. Illustrates a calmer workspace.

Each concept includes German and English icons and banners as SVG and PNG. Icons: 128×128 and 256×256. Banners: 772×250 and 1544×500. SVG files are standalone vectors with editable text; PNGs are rendered versions. Symbols remain the same across languages; short icon labels and banner copy are translated.

No concept is selected or activated as updater artwork yet. The designs are independent of the Elementor brand logo.
